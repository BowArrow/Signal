## Voluntary trial interview script

Use only with someone who agreed to discuss the product; no prospect email is authorized by this packet.

1. Tell me about the last time you compared two reports. What needed checking?
2. What did you use, and what was frustrating or risky?
3. Try your usual first task with non-sensitive sample files. Say what you expect as you go.
4. Which result would you verify before relying on it? Can you find that source?
5. Would this replace anything you already use? What would prevent you from paying for it?

Record observed behavior separately from promises or compliments. Ask permission for any follow-up. Track each placement and trial start date, then allow the full trial period before drawing purchase conclusions.
