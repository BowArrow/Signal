## Write a situation with a turn

Make the opening image and spoken line establish a concrete task or conflict immediately. Let the payoff introduce a surprising interpretation instead of announcing the joke's metaphor in advance. Write several possible turns and choose the one that is clear without the source video. Treat a reference's structure as inspiration, not permission to copy its script or footage.

Timing should follow the thought: allow comprehension and a reaction, then land the turn. Do not prescribe equal pauses after every sentence. BBC Maestro describes pacing, varied pauses, and delivery practice as parts of comic timing; a pre-punchline pause is not universally effective. [Comedic timing](https://www.bbcmaestro.com/blog/comedic-timing)
